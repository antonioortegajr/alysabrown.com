About the Artist

Alysa Brown is an artist based in Oregon. From a young age she always felt called to create. Though life led her down many different paths in pursuit of other endeavors, she eventually found her way back to her heart - art.

Her paintings capture the essence of her subjects through expressive brushstrokes and blending mediums. Often her technique involves layering with a combination of both charcoal and oil paint. While at times also incorporating acrylic paint and oil pastels.

As an artist, she draws inspiration from classical portraiture, impressionism, and contemporary abstract artwork. Blending these influences create her own unique aesthetic.

With her art, Alysa desires to enrich the lives of others, developing connections through paintings.
