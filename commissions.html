About commissions

A commission is a one of a kind artwork made just for you. I work from your favorite photos to capture a likeness, mood, and small details that make a subject yours. Paintings are such a beautiful way to commemorate what's important to you.

Commissions make meaningful gifts for holidays, anniversaries, birthdays, and memorials. There's just something extra special about receiving a piece of artwork meant just for you.

What I paint

  • Pets: dogs, cats, horses, and other animal companions, including memorial portraits.
  • Loved ones: parents, grandparents, siblings, couples, and wedding portraits.
  • People: individual portraits that capture personality, from candid moments to formal poses.
  • Places & keepsakes: homes, favorite spots, classic cars, and other things that hold a story.

Have an idea I did not mention? Let me know and I'd be happy to talk through any subject.
